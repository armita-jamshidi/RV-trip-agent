- 2026-10-10: The evaluation harness (T34) compares a plan with reference facts kept as data (`evals/reality/<trip>.json`, each fact with its source and check date) rather than fetching reality inside the scorer, so scoring is deterministic and the references can be collected by hand or by script wherever the sources are reachable. Tolerances: drive time within 15%, nightly price within $5, gas within 5%; hookups and site length must cover the trip's needs; a known bridge lower than the RV within 0.05 mi of a day's route fails unless that day warns about it. Reference collection is blocked here (routing, RIDB, recreation.gov, EIA and Overpass hosts all refused).
