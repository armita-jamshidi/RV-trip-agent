- 2026-10-16: `dave plan` (T35) runs a fixed pipeline in code (`dave.planner`: parse, RV size, a route the RV clears, days of at most 5 h, campgrounds, fuel, budget, booking proposals, then the validated render) instead of the open-ended agent loop, so every demo plan is complete, repeatable and checked by `validate_itinerary`. Place names are geocoded with OpenStreetMap Nominatim (free, no key, same data as OSRM and Overpass). Extra nights are spent at the destination; a round trip drives home after them. With no start date Dave plans two weeks out and says so. Missing keys leave parts out with a note rather than failing: no Foursquare key means no stops, food or gas stations; no RIDB key means no campgrounds, which the validator marks "Not final".
