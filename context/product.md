# Product

- **Buyer:** RV enthusiasts.
- **Pain:** Planning an RV trip is hard and overwhelming. The rig's size limits which roads, bridges and campsites work; every night needs a site with the right hookups; drive days must stay short; and costs add up fast.
- **Promise:** Based on your RV, budget and interests, Dave delivers a low-cost, fun RV road trip plan.

## What a plan contains
1. The user's RV identified, with dimensions from the manufacturer.
2. A route the RV fits under (bridge and tunnel clearances) with no day over 5 hours of driving.
3. One campground per night that is scenic, has both electric and water hookups, and has a site long enough for the RV.
4. Worthwhile stops matched to the user's interests (nature, museums, "largest X" landmarks).
5. Highly rated restaurants along the way.
6. Gas: cost estimate from current prices, and stations that have been cheapest in the area over the last 2 months.
7. Cost breakdown: fuel, campgrounds, tickets; total against budget.
8. Booking proposals for campgrounds and tickets, booked only after the user approves each one.
